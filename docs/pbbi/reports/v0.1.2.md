## What is behind the errors

1. **gpt-4.1-mini returns half the clauses and sometimes gives up**: BJB 4 of 110, SINARMAS 4 of 49, OCBC KK 17 of 233, SEABANK 42 of 358. Every reply ended normally, so the output cap is not the cause.
2. **v0.1.2 has the content but splits it differently.** Only 64 of 2,074 labelled clauses (3.1%) have no model clause covering ≥ 60% of their words. Lenient recall (≥ 90% of a clause's words inside one model clause) is 0.708. 230 model clauses merge two or more labelled clauses (SEABANK 66, SHOPEEPAY 43, DBS 28, OCBC IB 18, OCBC KK 17). BJB is the opposite: split too finely (175 vs 110).
3. **The labels group definitions two ways**: ranges (`DEFINISI|3-6`, opening sentence repeated) or one clause per term. In the 31 training contracts it is 13 vs 11, so there is no single rule. OCBC IB is the clearest case.
4. **Sampling at temperature 1.0.** The base `generation_config.json` sets `do_sample: true, temperature 1.0, top_k 20, top_p 0.95`, and the app sends no temperature, so output is sampled in test and production.
5. **Long contracts are harder**: recall 0.78 under 100 clauses vs about 0.6 above.
6. **The v0.1.1 heading-prefix problem is fixed**: 17 of 1,849 clauses (v0.1.1 on OVO: 326 of 345).
